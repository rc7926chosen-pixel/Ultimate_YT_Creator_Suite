Project Prompt: YouTube Creator Studio

Project Goal: To design and build an all-in-one, browser-based video editor that leverages AI and automation to streamline the workflow for YouTube content creators, from single clip edits to bulk content production.

Key Features and Functionality

1. Core Editing Suite

    Clip Trimming & Merging: A user-friendly timeline with intuitive click-and-drag functionality to easily trim, cut, and merge video and audio clips.

    Audio Controls: A dedicated track for audio with volume sliders, fade-in/out options, and the ability to add multiple audio layers.

2. YouTube-Specific Tools

    Image to Video Converter: A tool that combines an image (JPG, PNG) and an audio file (MP3, WAV) into a standard MP4 video, ideal for podcasts or music uploads with a static visual.

    Royalty-Free Asset Library: Integrate a curated library of YouTube-safe, royalty-free stock footage, images, and music directly within the editor to simplify content creation.

    Brand Kit: A feature to save and apply a channel's specific brand assets, including logos, a color palette, and custom fonts, for consistent branding across all videos.

3. AI-Powered Automation

    Automatic Subtitles & Text-Based Editing: An AI tool that automatically transcribes video audio into time-stamped subtitles. The user can then edit the video by directly editing the text transcript, with corresponding cuts made to the video clip.

    Silence Removal: An AI-driven function that detects and removes awkward pauses or periods of silence in the video, resulting in a more concise and professional final product.

    Smart Music Ducking: Automatically lowers the volume of background music when dialogue is present and raises it when the speaker stops, ensuring audio clarity.

4. Bulk Editing & Content Repurposing

    Bulk Template Creation: Enable users to create reusable templates (with predefined intros, outros, logo placements, and text styles) that can be applied to a large batch of videos simultaneously, ideal for channels producing many short-form clips.

    Cross-Platform Resizing: An intelligent reframing tool that automatically converts a standard 16:9 video to a 9:16 vertical format for YouTube Shorts or other social media platforms, with AI that keeps the main subject centered.

5. Post-Production & Publishing

    Enhanced Direct YouTube Integration: Connect to the user's YouTube account to not only upload the finished video but also automatically apply channel-specific settings, such as a video category. The app should also have the ability to suggest or auto-generate titles and descriptions based on the video's content.

    In-App Analytics Dashboard: A simple, integrated dashboard that provides a quick overview of the video's performance metrics (views, watch time, etc.) after it's been uploaded, encouraging continuous use of the platform.
